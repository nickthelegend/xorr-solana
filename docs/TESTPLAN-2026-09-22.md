     guard was skipped whenever they could not be read. Both fixed; grant now lands.
  3. The live site was two days stale: the web build wiped dist-web/.vercel, so every deploy
     after a rebuild silently created a NEW Vercel project. The Strategies tab, pre-IPO and
     hire-a-strategy were all merged and none were live.
  4. /prestocks — orphan of a rename, calling an endpoint that no longer exists.
  5. "the token's mint could not be read ()" — empty brackets in front of someone about to
     spend money.
  6. A blockhash lives ~55s; the permission screen is six paragraphs we want read. Both
     signers now retry once with a fresh blockhash.
